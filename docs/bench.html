03 · Culling

Why the defaults are not like for like

By default PlayCanvas skips splats drawn under a 1-pixel radius and, on WebGPU, splats whose opacity × area is under 0.75 px². Radix and Spark draw them, as the reference 3DGS rasterizer does. The culls are aggressive: on WebGPU they remove most of the bicycle's wheel spokes (section 04). PlayCanvas's dilation is also fixed at 0.075 px², a quarter of the 0.3 px² of standard 3DGS training, so its splats are drawn smaller than trained. So at defaults PlayCanvas does less work per frame, and on WebGPU Radix is 1.30× slower than it. With no culling in any renderer, Radix on WebGPU is 1.05× slower than PlayCanvas.

The same culling makes Radix on WebGPU 1.26× as fast as its defaults, through objectParams.minContribution, minPixelRadius, and params.minAlpha. PlayCanvas's thresholds are set in half pixels (it measures splats with twice the focal length), so its minContribution: 3 is 0.75 here.

05 · Known gaps

Where Radix is behind

  • Re-sort stalls on WebGL2. Radix's worker-sort cycle (a keys pass, a readback, then the order's upload) lands in rendered frames: 724 frames took over twice the median across the scenes at defaults, against 7 for Spark and PlayCanvas on WebGL2.
  • Loading. From the start of loading a PLY to the first frame: Radix 0.3 to 2.2 s, Spark 0.1 to 1.6 s, PlayCanvas 0.3 to 5.0 s.
06 · Method

How it was measured

  • Every renderer loads the same degree-0 PLY of each scan (Radix has no spherical harmonics) and draws the same camera poses: held still, a level turn, then a tilted turn, orbiting objects and looking around inside scenes.
  • Frames are drawn one at a time, each timed until the GPU has finished it, after a warm-up of at least 3 seconds. Each configuration ran 5 rounds in a shuffled order, in a fresh tab, with the laptop's thermal state and a canary configuration checked between runs.
  • The matched culling was checked on a grid of splats of known size and opacity: the renderers drew or culled the same splats, apart from a few right at a threshold.
